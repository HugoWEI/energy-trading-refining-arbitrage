# How to describe this project on a CV

**Use this wording only after personally reviewing and running the project.**

**Energy Trading & Refining Economics — Python Research Project (2026)**

- Built an EIA-sourced Python dashboard to evaluate 3-2-1 refining cracks, attribute crude/gasoline/diesel price changes and stress-test hypothetical transatlantic shipping differentials under freight and basis scenarios.

Avoid claiming profitable arbitrage, real freight intelligence, predictive alpha, trading returns or a rigorous backtest: the current research prototype establishes none of these.

## Likely BP trader questions

1. Why is Brent Europe minus WTI Cushing **not** an executable FOB/CIF shipping arbitrage?
2. Why is the gross crack not refinery net margin? What are the physical yield and grade limitations?
3. Which inventory draw, refinery outage, demand seasonality or policy event explains a specific weekly move? Cite dated sources.
4. How might one finance or hedge a time-lagged cargo shipment? What basis remains unhedged?
5. Why might LPG displace naphtha in an ethylene steam cracker, and what would change that substitution?
